The charge matters.
So does the person facing it.

A criminal case can affect your family, your work, and the life you are building. Jay handles the full range of Tennessee felony and misdemeanor matters. These are examples—not a complete list. If your charge is not listed, call to discuss it.

Assault & domestic violence

Simple assault, aggravated assault, domestic assault, and related allegations involving threats or violence.

Drug charges

Drug possession, sale, delivery, manufacturing, and possession with intent involving marijuana, cocaine, methamphetamine, fentanyl, and other controlled substances.

Theft, burglary & property crimes

Shoplifting, theft of property or services, burglary, robbery, vandalism, and related property allegations.

DUI & driving offenses

DUI, driving on a suspended or revoked license, reckless driving, and other misdemeanor or felony driving charges. Explore traffic-ticket help.

Weapons charges

Unlawful possession or carrying of a firearm and weapons allegations connected with other criminal charges.

Murder, including first-degree premeditated murder, attempted murder, manslaughter, kidnapping, and other serious violent allegations.

Sex offenses

Sexual assault, rape, and other sexual-offense allegations requiring careful review of the evidence and the accusation.

Fraud & other criminal matters

Forgery, fraud, financial offenses, public-order offenses, and other felony or misdemeanor allegations.

PREPARATION WITH A PURPOSE

Understand the facts.
Make informed decisions.

Your defense starts with listening to you and understanding the accusation. Jay’s work alongside John Dolan has included assisting with cases ranging from minor misdemeanors to first-degree premeditated murder. His approach centers on preparation and candid advice about the choices ahead.

01

Hear your side

Discuss what happened, what you have received from the court, and what matters most to you.

02

Examine the evidence

Review available reports, recordings, witness accounts, and other evidence. Identify questions and legal issues that need further work.

03

Discuss your options

Evaluate the strengths, weaknesses, and risks. Talk through negotiation and trial options without promises about the result.

04

Prepare the next step

Develop a plan for the stage your case is in, with attention to court dates, deadlines, and the work ahead.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you reach out.

Do you handle both felonies and misdemeanors?

Yes. Jay For Justice handles all types of Tennessee felony and misdemeanor charges in Memphis and Shelby County. Call with your specific charge, court, and case stage so Jay can assess the matter.

Can I contact Jay before I have been charged?

Yes. You can request an appointment about an investigation, a warrant, or concerns about a possible criminal charge. Explain any time-sensitive issue when you call.

Will my case go to trial?

That depends on the facts, evidence, available options, and decisions made in your case. Jay discusses negotiation and trial options with clients so they can make informed decisions. No particular outcome is guaranteed.

Can a family member request an appointment?

Yes. A family member may contact the firm for someone facing charges or in custody. The firm will confirm who the client is and discuss the engagement process.

What does it cost to hire Jay?

Fees depend on the charge, case stage, and work involved. Contact the firm to discuss your matter, the proposed scope of representation, and fees before deciding whether to hire Jay.
