COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you reach out.

Do you handle both divorce and parenting matters?

Yes. Contact Jay about divorce, parenting plans, custody, support, paternity, and questions about existing family-court orders. The firm will assess the issues and proposed scope of representation.

What if we agree on most things?

An agreement can be a useful starting point. Jay can review the issues, identify what still needs to be addressed, and discuss the documents and process involved.

Can an existing parenting or support order be changed?

That depends on the order, the circumstances, and the applicable legal requirements. Bring the current order and explain what has changed so Jay can evaluate the question.

What if I have already been served with papers?

Contact the firm promptly and provide the papers, the date you received them, and any hearing date you know. Whether Jay can take the matter depends on timing, conflicts, and the work required.

How much does a family-law case cost?

Fees depend on the issues, case stage, and work involved. The firm will discuss the proposed services, fees, and engagement terms before you decide whether to hire Jay.
